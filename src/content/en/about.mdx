# About

**IntD** is a small tool that demonstrates an **Int**eger **D**ecomposition algorithm.

Enter a target total and set each item's price range and last-digit rules. IntD then tries every possible combination and lists the top three results, ranked by your chosen preference: fewest plastic bags, or the most balanced item quantities.

In everyday life, you might wonder how a given total breaks down into several items (such as bentos and drinks) plus a one-dollar item (such as a plastic bag). **IntD** helps you find a few reasonable, natural combinations.

For the solving logic and complexity analysis, see the [algorithm notes](/en/algo).

In the Traditional Chinese version, the target amount is also spelled out in the formal capitalised numerals used on Taiwanese cheques and contracts (for example, 新臺幣…元整). This convention is specific to Chinese, so it does not appear here.
